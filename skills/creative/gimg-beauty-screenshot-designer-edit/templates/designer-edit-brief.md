## Goal

Improve the provided beauty App Store screenshots while preserving locked product, safety, and review boundaries. The main goals are usually: clearer visual hierarchy, cleaner beauty style, better text contrast, lower similarity between screenshots, and safer App Store review posture.

## What You May Change

- Background palette, texture, depth, and decoration density.
- Title/subtitle typography, line breaks, and wording polish when meaning is unchanged.
- Person crop/lighting/retouch within clean, non-adult, non-deceptive beauty style.
- Phone frame scale/shadow/position if the product screenshot remains readable.

## What You Must Not Change Without Approval

- Product UI content inside the device frame.
- App claims, ranking claims, price, privacy/security statements, or regulated wording.
- Export pixel dimensions and screenshot order.
- Any private user/account/review data.
- Anything that makes a person look underage, sexualized, unsafe, or misleading.
